**MimicCam™ is the mark of Ray Bekson (bigrayszn) — <https://github.com/bigrayszn/mimiccam>.**

The [GPL-2.0-or-later](LICENSE) covers the **code**. It does not cover the **name**, the
**icon**, or Ray Bekson's **identity** — a software licence never grants trademark
rights, and this policy is what makes that explicit. It is the same arrangement
Firefox, Signal and VLC use: the source is free, the brand is not, so a fork can never
be mistaken for the original.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what it is.** Refer to MimicCam by name in articles, videos, reviews, package
  descriptions, comparison tables and course material.
- **Say where it came from.** "Based on MimicCam by Ray Bekson", "a fork of MimicCam",
  "works with MimicCam" — with a link to the repository — is exactly right.
- **Redistribute an unmodified official build**, under its own name, with `NOTICE`,
  [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md) and [LICENSE](LICENSE) intact.
- **Fork the code, publish the fork, sell support for it** — under a *different name*.

## What you may not do

- **Ship a modified build under the name MimicCam**, or a name confusingly close to it:
  *MimicCam Pro*, *MimicCam+*, *Mimic-Cam*, *MimicCam AI*, *MimicCam 2* and the like.
  A fork that changes the app has to rename it.
- **Use the icon** — or a recoloured, cropped or redrawn version of it — for anything
  that is not an unmodified official build.
- **Imply affiliation or endorsement.** Do not state or suggest that a fork is
  official, approved, sponsored or supported by Ray Bekson, and do not use his name,
  handle or photo to promote it.
- **Register the name** — a domain, an app-store listing, a package name, a social
  handle, a company name or a trademark — for a product that is MimicCam or trades on
  the resemblance.
- **Strip the notices.** GPL-2.0 already forbids removing the copyright and licence
  notices from the code; this policy extends the same rule to `NOTICE`,
  [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md) and the author strings in `mimiccam/__init__.py`.

## If you fork it

1. **Rename** the app, the executable, the installer and the window title.
2. **Replace the icon** with artwork you own.
3. **Keep** `LICENSE`, `COPYRIGHT.md` and `NOTICE`, and keep the copyright headers in
   the source files — your fork's licence and the original author's copyright both
   have to be visible.
4. **State the change**, as GPL-2.0 §5 requires: a note that the build is modified and
   what was modified.

## Status of the mark

This is an **unregistered** claim (™), asserted by use in commerce since the first
public release. Registration is what upgrades it to ® and to a presumption of
ownership in a dispute; it is a filing with a national trademark office, typically one
class, and it is the single strongest step available beyond this document. Nothing in
this file depends on registration being granted — the claim, the notices and the
canonical repository URL are what a host or a court is asked to look at.

## If you find MimicCam somewhere else

The canonical project is **<https://github.com/bigrayszn/mimiccam>**. A download that
does not come from its **Releases** page, a mirror that rewrites the author, or a build
that calls itself MimicCam while behaving differently is not official.

Report it by opening an issue on the repository or messaging
[@bigrayszn](https://github.com/bigrayszn) on GitHub. A hosted copy that violates the
licence or these marks can be reported to the host through its copyright/trademark
process — GitHub's is <https://github.com/contact/dmca>.
